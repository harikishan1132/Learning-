STRW HAT PIRATES

Straw Hat Pirates is a fictional group of pirates in the anime and manga series One Piece. The crew is led by Monkey D. Luffy, who is on a quest to find the legendary treasure known as One Piece and become the Pirate King.

CAPTAIN - MONKEY D. LUFFY

Monkey D. Luffy is the captain of the Straw Hat Pirates
and dreams of finding the ultimate treasure known as the One Piece and
becoming the Pirate King.

CREW MEMBERS

RORONOA ZORO

Roronoa Zoro is vice captain of the Straw Hat Pirates
and wants to become the world's greatest swordsman.

Vinsmoke Sanji

Vinsmoke Sanji is the cook of the Straw Hat Pirates
and dreams of finding the All Blue, a legendary sea where all the fish from every ocean gather.
